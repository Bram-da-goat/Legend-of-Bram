Field guide & controls

WASD / arrows: move. Shift: run. E: interact. J: journal. Escape: close menus. Battles: click clear ground to place Bram; Space starts the wave. Q Earthshatter hits and stuns the leading pack anywhere on the road. X gives one paused reposition per battle. Use bends to hit enemies repeatedly.

Hammer: balanced reach. Axe: quick, narrow slashes and tree harvesting. Club: slow, powerful, huge AOE. Equip weapons from your inventory outside combat or during preparation. Goblins drop bones (10%); orcs drop tusks (25%); bats drop wings (30%) and extremely rare Vampire Shards (0.099%). Enemies return after you leave a cleared site alone for a while.
